01The Soulo Experience

Soulo is a Boston-based ensemble of musicians and producers creating distinctive live performances through jazz fusion, math rock, expressive improvisation, and electronic sound.

Our flexible lineup and imaginative arrangements bring an individual musical character to each occasion.

02Music as a Blessing

Inspired in part by its founder’s family heritage on Mount Wuzu in Huangmei, China, home to the temple of the Fifth Patriarch of Zen Buddhism, Soulo sees music as an offering of goodwill: a way to honor meaningful moments and wish others happiness.

04The Musicians

Soulo brings together musicians with backgrounds spanning Berklee College of Music, NYU, MIT, and Peking University, alongside jazz training and creative production.

What unites the ensemble is an adventurous approach to arrangement, sound, and live performance.

05What We Bring

  • Artful Live Arrangements

    Personalized interpretations, jazz-fusion textures, and expressive improvisation.

  • Flexible Ensembles

    Musicians and instrumentation selected to suit the atmosphere and scale of each event.

  • Creative Technology

    Synthesizers and live electronics are part of our musical language. We are exploring new ways to bring creative technology into live performance.
